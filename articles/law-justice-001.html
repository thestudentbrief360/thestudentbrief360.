Should Lawyers Be Allowed To Use Artificial Intelligence In Their Briefs?

By Arabella O´Reilly • 2026 • Opinion Brief

With the fast growth of AI and new advances coming into the mainstream everyday it is logical to question where and how AI should be used. Lawyers should not be allowed to use artificial intelligence in their briefs because artificial intelligence is still new so there is not enough training to use it correctly and we do not fully understand the ethics of Artificial intelligence and has been proven to obtain not the best accuracy.

The reason for this stance is not everyone understands AI yet and how to use it correctly, for example according to the Pew Research Center they state that ¨in the new survey 62% of americans use ai on a daily basis¨ that is more than half of americans and it can be assumed that in that group there are some people that might not have access to proper training and the skills to use it correctly so why should firms provide and allow the use of AI to our lawyers without proper training. Survey show that about 70 to 79 % of lawyers use AI for their job on a daily basis and according to the Bloomberg law report ¨80% of legal professionals have access to AI tools with less than 30% percent being highly confident in the use AI¨ so that means 50 % of legal professional are less confident leading to misconceptions and mistakes.

A second reason why lawyers should not be able to use AI in their briefs is because we do not understand the full ethics of AI, ethics in ai are the principle set for AI to make sure it is safety to use these are such as deep understanding of humans and morals from what is wrong from right so that people can use AI in the safest way possible without bias. The problem with AI is it is so fast growing there has to be someone making the principles but AI has the power to analyze massive amounts of data to learn new skills on a daily basis and can fix its own knowledge gaps in real time which something we haven't seen in any other technology before so making it very hard to keep track of the ethics due to how fast pace it is coupled with lack of research.

This presents a problem and a big negative impact especially for firms and lawyers all across the world because AI makes more room for inaccuracy and error in cases and presents an issue of misuse which could be a problem if used in the wrong way and put in the wrong hands. For example if a criminal trial occurs and a lawyer used AI on either the plaintiffs or defendants side that means there could be an error which would make a tear in the justice system, a system that hasn't always been exactly fair and could even result in a wrongful conviction putting innocent people behind bars. Overall it has been made clear that lawyers should not be allowed to use artificial intelligence in their briefs because artificial intelligence is still new so there is not enough training to use it correctly and we do not fully understand the ethics of Artificial intelligence and has been proven to obtain inaccuracy. So get out there and learn more about AI, so that together we can protect our future.
